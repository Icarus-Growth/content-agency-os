# Clip rules

The AI reads these rules before it picks clips. Change them to match your own style.

## A good short clip

1. It holds one complete idea. A person who has not seen the long video understands it.
2. It starts strong: a claim, a question, a number or a problem. It does not start with
   "so", "and", "but" or "like I said".
3. It ends on a full sentence. It does not stop in the middle of a thought.
4. It is between the shortest and the longest length you set (20 to 60 seconds by default).
5. It has a clear payoff: a tip, a story with an end, a strong opinion or a surprise.

## Leave out

1. Greetings, sign-offs and "like and subscribe".
2. Parts that need a picture on screen the viewer cannot see in the clip.
3. Parts that point to "what I said before".
4. Two clips that tell the same idea. Pick the stronger one.

## For each clip, give

- the start and the end, in seconds, on the edges of whole sentences;
- a short title (at most 8 words);
- the first line a viewer hears (the hook);
- why it works, in one sentence.
