# Hook rules

The AI reads these rules before it scores an opening. Change them to match your own
style. Keep each rule to one short line.

## What a strong opening does

1. It makes one clear promise or one clear problem in the first sentence.
2. It speaks to one person ("you"), not to "everyone".
3. It gives a reason to keep watching in the first 3 seconds (about 8 to 10 words).
4. It uses plain, spoken words. It sounds like a person talking, not an advert.
5. It is specific: a number, a result, a time, a place or a named mistake.
6. It opens a loop: the answer comes later in the video.

## What makes an opening weak

1. A greeting or a name first ("Hi guys", "Welcome back", "My name is").
2. A slow start: context or background before the point.
3. A vague claim with no detail ("This will change everything").
4. A question the viewer can answer "no" to.
5. Clickbait the video does not pay off.

## How to score (1 to 10)

- 9 or 10: stops the scroll. Clear, specific and it opens a loop in under 10 words.
- 7 or 8: good. One small fix makes it strong.
- 5 or 6: works, but it is slow or vague.
- 3 or 4: weak. The point comes too late.
- 1 or 2: no hook. A greeting, or nothing that makes a person stay.

## Rewrites

- Keep the meaning and the facts of the video. Never add a claim the video does not make.
- Keep it short enough to say in 3 seconds.
- Write in the same language as the opening.
